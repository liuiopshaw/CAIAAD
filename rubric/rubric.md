## Six-Dimension Evaluation Framework (Neutral Version)

> Revised 2026-09-16: all subjective bonus content removed — no dosage form is highlighted anymore (the former "highlight nanformulation advantages" framework and the per-dimension preset-score examples have been deleted). Scoring relies only on the objective anchors of each dimension; the therapeutic type itself is not a reason for bonus or penalty.

| Dimension | Weight | Core question |
|:---|:---:|:---|
| **0. AD relevance (gating dimension)** | Gating | Is the therapeutic genuinely relevant to AD treatment? |
| **1. Target-tissue delivery efficiency** | 30% | Can the therapeutic effectively reach its site of action? |
| **2. Multi-target synergy potential** | 15% | Can the therapeutic intervene in multiple pathological aspects of AD simultaneously? |
| **3. Effect duration** | 10% | Is the effect transient or sustained? |
| **4. Manufacturing QC & precise tunability** | 25% | Is its preparation controllable, scalable, and precisely tunable in composition and dose? |
| **5. Biosafety** | 20% | What is the risk-benefit ratio at the effective dose? |

> **Overall score formula (additive)**: overall = dim1 x 30% + dim2 x 15% + dim3 x 10% + dim4 x 25% + dim5 x 20%.
> Dimension 0 "AD relevance" is recorded for reference only and does not enter the overall score. (Two variants are kept for experiments, selected via parameter: hard gate x (dim0/10) and soft gate x (0.5 + 0.5 x dim0/10).)

### Dimension 0: AD relevance (gating, not weighted)

**Core question**: Is the therapeutic genuinely relevant to AD treatment?

| Score | Criterion |
|:---|:---|
| 9-10 | Approved for the AD indication, or proven efficacy in AD patients |
| 7-8 | Clinical-stage candidate for AD (Phase II/III), or strong preclinical AD evidence |
| 5-6 | Preclinical AD evidence, or mechanism explicitly targeting an AD pathological pathway |
| 3-4 | Only indirectly/speculatively related to AD (e.g. general antioxidant, general anti-inflammatory), no AD-specific evidence |
| 1-2 | Drug/compound unrelated to AD |

> **Score anchor examples**:
> - **Donepezil**: approved first-line AD therapy -> **9-10**
> - **Anti-A beta monoclonal antibody (clinical stage)**: clinical candidate targeting core AD pathology -> **7-8**
> - **Unrelated drugs such as vitamin C**: general antioxidant, no AD-specific evidence -> **1-2**

### Dimension 1: Target-tissue delivery efficiency (weight 30%)

**Core question**: Can the therapeutic effectively reach its site of action?

| Score | Criterion |
|:---|:---|
| 9-10 | Efficiently reaches target tissue with a well-defined administration route, and delivery efficiency can be optimized by design |
| 7-8 | Effectively reaches target tissue with a well-defined administration route |
| 5-6 | Partially reaches target tissue, but with some delivery bottlenecks |
| 3-4 | Faces major delivery challenges |
| 1-2 | No viable delivery strategy yet |

### Dimension 2: Multi-target synergy potential (weight 15%)

**Core question**: Can the therapeutic intervene in multiple pathological aspects of AD simultaneously?

| Score | Criterion |
|:---|:---|
| 9-10 | Intervenes in >= 3 core AD pathological pathways simultaneously, with evidence of synergy |
| 7-8 | Intervenes in 2 core AD pathological pathways |
| 5-6 | Mainly targets 1 pathological pathway |
| 3-4 | Targets only a single target |
| 1-2 | Target unclear |

### Dimension 3: Effect duration (weight 10%)

**Core question**: Is the effect produced by the therapeutic transient or sustainable?

| Score | Criterion |
|:---|:---|
| 9-10 | A single/short-term intervention produces long-term biological effects |
| 7-8 | Effect lasts weeks to months |
| 5-6 | Effect duration is limited |
| 3-4 | Effect is transient and rapidly fades after discontinuation |
| 1-2 | No evidence of lasting effect |

### Dimension 4: Manufacturing QC & precise tunability (weight 25%)

**Core question**: Is its preparation controllable, scalable, and precisely tunable in composition and dose?

| Score | Criterion |
|:---|:---|
| 9-10 | Well-defined chemical composition, controllable synthesis, batch consistency, precisely tunable dose, easy to scale up |
| 7-8 | Relatively well-defined, controllable, scalable composition |
| 5-6 | Complex but controllable composition; scaling up is challenging |
| 3-4 | Ill-defined composition or highly donor/bio-sourced, large batch variation, difficult to precisely control |
| 1-2 | Uncontrollable, not scalable |

### Dimension 5: Biosafety (weight 20%)

**Core question**: What is the risk-benefit ratio at the effective dose?

| Score | Criterion |
|:---|:---|
| 9-10 | Mild, controllable side effects |
| 7-8 | Moderate side effects, monitorable and manageable |
| 5-6 | Some safety concerns |
| 3-4 | Clear safety risks |
| 1-2 | Serious safety problems |
